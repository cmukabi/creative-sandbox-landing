How it works

From passage to published in three moves

The Creative Sandbox slides in beside any page, so the spark and the draft live side by side.

  1. Highlight & sendSelect a passage, right-click Send to Creative Sandbox — or tap the toolbar icon for a blank sprint.
  2. Pick a card & write forwardA 30-minute sprint starts. Backspace is locked, so you keep moving instead of editing.
  3. Polish & launchUnlock the Polish Box, tidy the draft, then export it in one click.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need an AI key?

No. Every card runs on built-in scaffolds and local templates. An OpenAI or Anthropic key adds full AI drafting and polishing; an ElevenLabs key adds sung tracks.

Why can't I use Backspace?

That's the point of the sprint: you write forward. When the timer ends, the Polish Box unlocks and you can edit freely.

Which poetic forms are supported?

Free Verse, Sonnet, Haiku, Limerick and Villanelle — each with its rules on screen and live line, syllable and refrain checks.

Where does it work?

On almost any website in Google Chrome. Chrome doesn't allow extensions on a few protected pages, such as the Chrome Web Store and chrome:// settings pages.
